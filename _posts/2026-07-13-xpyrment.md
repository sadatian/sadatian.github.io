A low-code Python library designed for enterprise-grade experiment design, classical Design of Experiments (DoE), and advanced statistical analysis. Features include power analysis, CUPED variance reduction, Sample Ratio Mismatch (SRM) checks, Welch's t-test, Delta method ratio metrics, random-effects meta-analysis, Simonsohn p-curve distribution audits, and automated standalone HTML reporting.
